    How a loan gets done

    Four steps, no surprises.

    01 / 04
    1

    Start with a number.

    Text me a scenario or run it yourself in the toolkit. In about fifteen minutes you'll know the payment, the cash to close and which loan types fit.

    2

    Get pre-approved.

    I review income, assets and credit up front, so the letter you hand a seller holds up when it counts.

    3

    I shop the loan.

    Once you're in contract I price your loan across wholesale lenders and show you the options side by side: rate, points and total cost.

    4

    Close on time.

    You and your agent get updates at every milestone. You see final numbers before signing day, not at the table.

    Have renters with a lease ending soon?

    Send me the names and I'll reach out for you, with a rent-versus-own comparison built on their numbers.
