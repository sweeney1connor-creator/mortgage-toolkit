Prop 37 · looks set to pass

3% down on a newly built home

The state would lend up to 17% of the price as a fixed-rate second loan. Here is the down payment on a $750,000 new build.

  • Clients with good income and thin savings become new-construction buyers, so builder relationships matter more.
  • Entry-level resale listings may face more competition from new builds.
  • The second loan has its own payment, so buyers need to qualify on both.

Prop 1, also likely to pass: an $11.25 billion housing bond. $1.25 billion would go to veteran housing and the rest to rental, homeownership and other affordable housing programs. Expect a slower effect, over several years.

California insurance is easing

The good newsNine insurers, including six of the ten largest, have committed to write more California home policies. It is the first real growth in three years.
The catchFire zones are still tight. The FAIR Plan holds about 697,000 policies, and its rates rose an average of 29% in October.
Your moveThe premium counts toward the payment a buyer qualifies on. Get a quote in the first days of escrow so it does not sink the loan late.
